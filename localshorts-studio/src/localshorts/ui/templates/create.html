Pipeline Architecture & Quality Gates
Every Short passes 18 automated stages before entering `ready-to-upload/`
1. Script & Hook 55–80 words (`20–45s`), bans generic AI intros, generates 3 hooks + curiosity cliff + concrete payoff.
2. Originality Gate 3-gram Jaccard + RapidFuzz + TF-IDF cosine check (`< 0.78`) against source headlines and last 90 days of local scripts.
3. Scene Planner 8–15 dynamic visual beats (`2.0–4.0s` each), enforces adjacent visual-type diversity and 2–5 punch zooms.
4. Local CPU TTS Clause-level WAV synthesis + voice-first mixing (`-14 LUFS` voice, `-24 dB` SFX ducking, `-1.0 dBTP` soft limiter).
5. Kinetic Captions 2–5 words per card, automatic yellow/cyan emphasis on numbers & power verbs, burned in via `.ass` + exported `.srt`.
6. NVENC / CPU Render `1080×1920` 30fps `yuv420p` MP4 via `h264_nvenc` (or `libx264` fallback) after unloading Ollama VRAM.
7. Technical & Content QC Verifies resolution, audio stream, freeze frames, and scores 0–100 across Hook, Retention, Originality, Audio, Visuals, and Captions (`>= 70` required for `READY`).
